What's new Integrations Supported thermostats

One app, every OS

One self-contained executable for Windows, macOS and Linux, recovery firmware included. Connect the thermostat with a USB data cable.

More from the thermostat you already own.

Connect to Home Assistant, keep control on your network, and make it yours.

No cloud

MQTT, Matter or Zigbee replaces the thermostat's Nest cloud connection with nestlocal, a service on the unit. The Nest and Google Home apps no longer control it. No Google account, no internet.

MQTT

The thermostat connects to your MQTT broker; Home Assistant discovers it there. Modes, setpoints, eco, humidity, fan, schedule and more.

Matter

A Matter thermostat over Wi-Fi, paired with a code or, on supported 3rd generation units, over Bluetooth. Thread is experimental.

Zigbee

A Zigbee router on the thermostat's own 802.15.4 radio. Sleeps between messages.

On-device settings

An UNFR0ZEN menu on the thermostat: Matter pairing code and QR, Zigbee pairing. The original nlclient is kept for undo.

External temperature

Any thermometer in Home Assistant regulates the heating in place of the thermostat's own sensor, over MQTT, Matter or Zigbee. 2nd and 3rd generation.

Backup & restore

Back up and restore the whole NAND as an image, from the app or the CLI.

It runs Doom

Obviously. Turn the ring to walk, press to fire. The shareware episode is included.

MQTT, Matter and Zigbee install nestlocal on the thermostat, which takes the place of Nest's cloud: the Nest and Google Home apps no longer control the unit. MQTT and Matter use Wi-Fi, joined from Settings → Network; the network doesn't need internet access.

External temperature: the thermostat regulates from a reading sent over MQTT, Matter or Zigbee, any thermometer in Home Assistant for example. Nest Temperature Sensors are not supported: the option turns the 3rd generation's Bluetooth off, and the 2nd generation never had them. See Integrations.
