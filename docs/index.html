More from the thermostat you already own

Keep working hardware useful. The line to Nest's cloud comes out, and three go in to Home Assistant on your own network.

  • Cloud

    Disconnected

    nestlocal, a service on the thermostat, takes its place. The Nest and Google Home apps no longer control the unit. No Google account, no internet.

  • MQTT

    Wi-Fi, to your MQTT broker

    Home Assistant discovers the thermostat there. Modes, setpoints, eco, humidity, fan, schedule and more.

  • Matter

    Wi-Fi, to a Matter controller

    Paired with a code or, on supported 3rd generation units, over Bluetooth.

  • Zigbee

    The thermostat's own 802.15.4 radio, to your coordinator

    A Zigbee router for Zigbee2MQTT or ZHA.

  • Temperature

    A reading sent over MQTT, Matter or Zigbee

    Any thermometer in Home Assistant regulates the heating in place of the thermostat's own sensor.

One app, every OS

One self-contained executable for Windows, macOS and Linux, recovery firmware included. Connect the thermostat with a USB data cable.
